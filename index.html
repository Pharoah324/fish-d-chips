Meet the chef behind the batter

Our head chef grew up frying fish in London's classic chippies before bringing the recipe stateside. At Fish D Chips, he combined that time-honored beer batter with an idea nobody else in Atlanta was doing: let guests choose their own fish, fried in the style they crave — from classic London beer batter to Southern cornmeal crunch. It's a new concept built on an old craft.
